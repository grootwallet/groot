# Supplied security-report triage — 2026-10-03

Status: static source-impact triage of three supplied v0.4.96 reports against
commit `f7b4b993`. This is not a dynamic pentest, hosted-service assessment, or
physical-device certification.

Release reconciliation (2026-10-04): the owner confirmed that v0.4.96's full
certification evidence existed but had not been reflected in the repository.
ADR 0053 and the completed checklist now authorize that exact commit. The
remaining finding verdicts and approval boundaries below are unchanged.

The product owner required explicit approval before functional changes. On
2026-10-04 the owner approved decision-queue items 2, 3, 5, 6, 13, and 15 plus
automatic failure-atomic secure-store v5 migration. ADR 0081 records that scope;
item 12 and every other proposal remain deferred.

Verdicts use `confirmed`, `not_actionable`, and `needs_review`. “Confirmed” does
not mean approved for implementation; the approval column remains controlling.
