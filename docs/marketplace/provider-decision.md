# Provider decision after the alternatives review

Recovery note, 17 November 2026: this document is preserved research. Later discussion left provider qualification unresolved; see the [recovery scope](reconciliation.md#provider-evaluation-remains-unresolved). The existing development adapter is not a production-provider decision.

Updated 11 November 2026 for optional onchain bitcoin/EUR/SEPA Instant buy and sell inside Groot.

## Decision

**The selected live product is live offers with in-app trade execution.** Qualify LocalCoinSwap first as the provider for liquidity, trade coordination, escrow and dispute decisions. Groot guides each buyer or seller through their part of the trade, from review through settlement; the buyer makes the actual fiat transfer in their bank. A source link or provider website trade is useful for research and recovery, but does not meet the selected product outcome. LocalCoinSwap remains a candidate and the existing local discovery source. Do not add Lightning, combine books, or take on Groot-operated escrow to make a provider fit.

Prepare the marketplace as a separate **0.5.5** release after the Miniscript and mobile-pairing wallet release ships, subject to [production readiness](production-readiness.md). This selection supersedes the earlier handoff-first product recommendation. The [development gateway](localcoinswap-api.md) implements live public offers and scoped access to an existing account's trades, chat and selected actions. Completing BTC execution still requires a dedicated native protocol and recovery design.

This replaces the initial Hodl Hodl preference. Its current terms restrict API use for competing products and other uses relevant to Groot. LocalCoinSwap explicitly invites custom interfaces and publishes its public and account API. The documentation we reviewed does not state that separate written permission is required to integrate. A bounded BTC/EUR/SEPA Instant public read returned listings. Verify actual country eligibility, costs, completed volume and production escrow for the intended rollout. The [current API](https://localcoinswap.com/openapi.json), [official integration invitation](https://github.com/LocalCoinSwap/LocalCoinSwap), [terms](https://localcoinswap.com/terms-of-service) and [Bitcoin protocol reference](https://github.com/LocalCoinSwap/bitcoin-trading-scripts) must be read together. ## What exists and what the selected product needs

- Keep the synthetic full buy/sell journey for reviewing the intended experience. It never accepts an advertisement as a real trade or moves wallet state.
- The separate public-listing research page uses one source, EUR and SEPA Instant. A development flag plus explicit per-visit consent gates reads. No automatic startup, unlock, Settings, entry or background requests.
- The optional account gateway uses its own loopback launcher, defaults to fixtures and supports live public offers, existing trades, text chat, payment claims and dispute actions in the configured modes. It has a separate receipt journal and exposes no BTC execution. See the [API contract](localcoinswap-api.md).
- Use portable public-offer DTOs and isolated normalizers. Provider selection is internal configuration, not another choice the customer has to understand. Never combine books, silently route or fall back to another provider.
- Keep the euro amount local for LocalCoinSwap: its documented search has no amount filter. The provider receives the development server's public IP and the requested pair/method/direction. It receives no wallet, bank or account data from Groot.
- Normalize native BTC, current payment-method slug, side, exact decimal prices, integer fiat bounds, active/public flags and last-seen freshness. Exclude unsupported fixed-size, phone-verification and minimum-reputation requirements under the newcomer research policy. Unknown eligibility stays unknown.
- Preserve provider decimal precision for sorting and satoshi indications with integer ratios. A rounded displayed euro price is not used for accounting. Advertisements omit final platform/mining/banking fees; show gross indications only, without a “best total” or net-receipt promise.
- Opening the source offer remains a separate confirmed external information action in research. It sends no Groot order or payout address, accepts no terms and creates no trade. Review data use and attribution for the intended production distribution.
- An in-app trade starts only after the user reviews and explicitly accepts exact, current provider terms with validated amount, rail, counterparty and fees. Only Rust may bind the current wallet to a labelled destination and authorize signing or broadcast. Keep authenticated trade state, messages and evidence isolated from the wallet's general activity and the synthetic service. Show escrow funding and Bitcoin confirmations separately from the buyer's fiat-payment claim and the seller's independently checked receipt. Keep the provider's dispute channel accessible from the trade, including after restart or when new trading is paused.

The current sandbox and this decision change no native command, key, transaction, wallet/profile/backup/proposal/registry format, dependency, production network origin or release authorization. **BIP impact: none** for this decision. Native execution requires its own ADR, threat model, standards assessment and release evidence.

## What the official API proves

The [LocalCoinSwap v2 OpenAPI](https://localcoinswap.com/openapi.json) documents unauthenticated `GET /api/v2/offers/search/`, then separately scoped account endpoints. `POST /api/v2/trades/non-custodial/create/` takes an offer and documents a provider web-wallet address; `PATCH /api/v2/trades/non-custodial/update/{uuid}/` advances trade state. Trade detail, chat and dispute open/withdraw operations are documented. API-key status polling is limited to two requests per minute per account; website WebSocket feeds are unavailable to API keys.

The same specification documents wallet-scoped `GET /api/v2/trade-utxos/BTC/` for sweeping completed escrow outputs and `POST /api/v2/wallets/publish/` for signed BTC broadcasts. The general signing guide covers smart-contract chains, and the unsigned-transaction endpoint excludes BTC. The provider's [Bitcoin script reference](https://github.com/LocalCoinSwap/bitcoin-trading-scripts) describes hashed participant and dispute secrets; it is not proof of the current production script or a complete native signing recipe. Neither the API nor the reference establishes a supported BTC sandbox, a current independent key/recovery path, or direct escrow payout to a fresh Groot address. Ask for these explicitly. Do not import a wallet mnemonic, decrypt trade keys on a Groot server, or use the `wallet` scope merely to browse or take offers.

## Gates before real trading

1. **Provider scope and operating terms.** Review contracting entity, intended jurisdictions, branding, per-user onboarding, key scopes, supported BTC test environment, exact costs, own-wallet payouts, limits, mutation and retry semantics, evidence, dispute coverage and shutdown recovery. Agree referral or other commercial terms if the product uses them.
2. **Operational depth.** Use documented public limits to sample eligible unique counterparties at €50, €100 and €250 on both sides for 14 days. Track geographic and newcomer restrictions, availability, spread and concentration. Measure executed volume, all-in costs and settlement in separate budgeted exercises after protocol and eligibility checks.
3. **Protocol and recovery proof.** Review the current secret/preimage escrow lifecycle, buyer/seller/provider powers, transaction paths, dispute branches and lost-device/operator-offline outcomes. Specify native storage and recovery before collecting or deriving trade secrets. Do not reuse wallet seeds or the existing standard multisig UI as a shortcut.
4. **Apply the agreed compliance scope.** Record which entities, countries, services, payments and distribution channels the owner's clearance covers. Verify the actual provider entity and service scope for those countries. Document who submits requests, controls trade keys, decides disputes and retains evidence for this in-app path.
5. **Native candidate.** Dedicated bounded native transport, authoritative funding/release review, new permanently labelled destination per trade, durable idempotent journal/reconciliation, independent security review and exact-candidate release checklist. Any persisted-format change follows the repository's compatibility/approval rules.

If LocalCoinSwap cannot support the complete reviewed BTC lifecycle, keep the live feature disabled. Evaluate a negotiated Hodl Hodl partnership against the same requirements. A provider website trade or a Bisq Easy buy-first product requires a separate product decision, because it changes the selected experience or trust model.
