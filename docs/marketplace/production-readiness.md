## What local acceptance establishes

The durable service has its own private repository, `grootwallet/groot-marketplace`, with sandbox-only execution. `pnpm test:marketplace-backend` runs three service passes and repeated actual-service browser acceptance. `pnpm test:marketplace-deployment` separately builds an inventoried distribution and exercises its actual TLS proxy, private access, Secure cookies, denied routes/outbound routing, restart persistence and idempotency, followed by two browser passes per engine/layout. Backup/restore tools validate the existing service format and require fresh login after restore.

The separate LocalCoinSwap fixture suite, `pnpm test:marketplace-provider`, exercises the HTTP gateway, provider normalization, status checks and operation journal. A live public search and detail read exercised the fixed provider origin. Fixture account tests cover HTTP requests and responses. Real account writes, fiat receipt, executable liquidity and native BTC escrow require their own evidence. [Gateway contract and testing instructions](localcoinswap-api.md)

The demo supports synthetic EUR/SEPA Instant buy/sell at €50–250, six exercises, explicit restart and disposable state. Unavailable offers have one inline **Restore demo offers** action. Direction/offer selections expose their pressed state; optional explanations use disclosures. Cancelled, refund and disputed outcomes retain their actual status without a linear progress indicator implying payment occurred. Demo, receipt, release and expiry warnings remain visible where they affect a decision.

Automated acceptance must cover complete buy/sell paths, delayed payment, fresh seller acknowledgement, pending refund and late claim, pre-funding cancellation, offline recovery, expired quotes, stale async results, restart/leave/reload and translated desktop/mobile layouts. Synthetic runs forbid external HTTP/WebSocket traffic and public research lookups. Discovery tests use intercepted responses and reject unintended provider requests; they do not execute a provider trade.

Run the isolated repeat acceptance command with the pinned runtime and installed Chromium/WebKit browsers:

```sh
pnpm_config_verify_deps_before_run=false pnpm verify:marketplace
```

It defaults to two consecutive browser acceptance passes across Chromium and WebKit, each with desktop/mobile projects, after formatting, types, fixtures, units and build. It fails on check failure, incomplete/skipped/retried browser cases, source drift or attempted upstream requests. It writes a source manifest, machine-readable report, logs and screenshots under `outputs/marketplace-readiness/`; browser case totals come from collection rather than a historical constant. It supplements mandatory `pnpm validate` and applicable release checks. Do not edit source or run another sync/build while it owns the workspace/server.

The [marketplace fixture workflow](../../.github/workflows/marketplace-verification.yml) runs the same default command automatically on pull requests and pushes to `main`. It installs exact locked dependencies and the corresponding Chromium/WebKit browsers, keeps failed fixture evidence for seven days, and requires no live provider, funds, service account or deployment secret. Existing CI and release checks remain independent requirements. A configured workflow is not evidence of a successful hosted run; inspect its exact candidate and reports before citing a pass.

The last recorded `pnpm validate` is **not green**: the existing guard reports that `src-tauri/src/session.rs` changed after the reviewed Mainnet policy snapshot. Marketplace work does not modify that file or refresh reviewed hashes. This release blocker must be resolved through its owning source-review process, independently of a passing marketplace run. See the [verification record](rehearsal-verification.md).

Browser fixtures establish presentation and deterministic orchestration, including keyboard submit/review, modal focus containment/return, reduced motion and a 320×568 viewport. Physical iOS behavior, actual 200% zoom, executable liquidity, fiat settlement, escrow security and release authorization require independent evidence.

## Launch gates

Qualify **LocalCoinSwap for provider-backed in-app execution** before changing the sandbox or enabling real trades. A confirmed website link can remain a source inspection or recovery path; opening it cannot satisfy this release's buy/sell acceptance. LocalCoinSwap must retain provider escrow coordination and dispute decisions. Groot verifies what it authorizes and never takes the provider escrow role. Users make fiat transfers in their own bank and sellers confirm actual receipt before authorizing release.

Apply the owner's compliance clearance to the exact entity, countries and embedded trade roles covered. Separately verify LocalCoinSwap's provider-side rights for that scope. The [16 February 2026 CNMV warning](https://www.cnmv.es/webservices/verdocumento/ver?e=sgvrJvcRF3Z50jKASYyDc5qsmhmHlGKVzGKYDPXP7jQPj57IXgF7VdIcuAns0KKL) names LocalCoinSwap. A search of both [ESMA interim register files dated 5 November 2026](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica#interim-mica-register) found no LocalCoinSwap or TechHouse name/domain match; that limited search neither settles authorization nor supersedes the dated warning. Confirm the contracting entity and applicable service coverage alongside the provider agreement.

## Smallest eventual human acceptance

Use automated local evidence for fixture regression. After reviewing legal scope, platforms and the funded budget, record these human acceptance outcomes.

1. **Comprehension and informed acceptance:** on each proposed launch platform, one new participant identifies the provider, data shared, binding price and fees, separate bank action and dispute owner before taking a real offer. Back or cancel before confirmation creates no trade.
2. **One authorized in-app buy and one in-app sell:** record the reviewed fiat amount, complete fees, recipient and actual chain result. The seller independently checks matching bank receipt before release. Reconcile expected versus actual fiat/satoshi totals; a payment claim alone never counts as receipt or completion. Website-only execution does not count.
3. **Recovery and operator exercise:** prove restart and ambiguous-mutation recovery on the exact native candidate without duplicate funding or release. Exercise the provider's recovery and support path plus delayed payment, refund and dispute in its authenticated sandbox. Each has an identified owner, durable status and response target.

These individual checks do not replace the corridor cohort's coverage/completion targets, independent security review, physical-device evidence or exact-release authorization. Any failed gate keeps real trading unavailable; narrow the corridor or defer instead of filling gaps with simulated inventory.

Compatibility and standards: this readiness work changes no persisted wallet/profile/backup/proposal/registry format and requires no migration. The isolated service v1 journal is already covered by ADR 0114; operations tooling preserves that format and restores without active sessions. **No BIP impact:** no derivation, descriptor, transaction, PSBT, protocol or native signing behavior changes. A future live escrow protocol needs its own ADR, compatibility plan, threat model and approval where required.
